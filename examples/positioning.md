## 1. 一句话定位 / One-Sentence Positioning

对应模板 / Matching the reference formula:

> `[Project Name]` is a `[product/service category]` for `[target customer]`, helping them solve `[specific pain point]` through `[core technology/workflow]` and achieve `[business outcome]`.

> `[项目名称]` 是面向 `[目标客户]` 的 `[产品/服务类型]`，通过 `[核心技术/工作流]` 解决 `[具体痛点]`，帮助客户实现 `[业务结果]`。

### 填写示例 / Filled-in example

> 智汇学伴（StudyPal AI）是面向一二线城市、由家长付费的初高中学生家庭（主楔子）的 AI 学习陪伴工具：相较于搜题工具「主要给答案」与一对一辅导「时段绑定」，它通过**引导式对话**和**知识图谱诊断**，在课后卡题时提供即时、可引导学生思考的辅导，帮助学生理解概念、梳理薄弱环节并获得学习路径 / 练习建议。付费转化、诊断准确率对比与规模化推荐效果均 `待验证`。

> StudyPal AI (智汇学伴) is an AI study-companion tool for first- and second-tier city middle- and high-school households where the parent pays (primary wedge). Unlike answer-search tools that mainly deliver answers and one-to-one tutoring bound to scheduled sessions, it uses **guided dialogue** and **knowledge-graph diagnosis** to give immediate, thinking-oriented help when students are stuck after school—so they can understand concepts, map weak points, and get learning-path / practice suggestions. Paid conversion, accuracy comparisons, and scale effects of recommendations remain `待验证`.
