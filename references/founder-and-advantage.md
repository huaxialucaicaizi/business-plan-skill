# Founder And Advantage Reference

Use this reference to draft founder/team and competitive-advantage sections without inventing facts.

## Founder Profile Structure

Founder intro formula:

> `[Founder Name]`, `[education/background]`, has `[years]` years of experience in `[relevant fields]`. Previously worked on `[relevant products/projects]`, with strengths in `[capability 1]`, `[capability 2]`, and `[capability 3]`. In this project, the founder is responsible for `[role in venture]`.

Chinese formula:

> `[创始人姓名]`，`[学历/背景]`，拥有 `[年限]` 年 `[相关领域]` 经验。曾参与 `[相关项目/产品]`，擅长 `[能力1]`、`[能力2]` 和 `[能力3]`。在本项目中主要负责 `[职责]`。

If the user has not provided proof of a claim, soften it:
- Use `具备相关经验` instead of exact quantified outcomes.
- Use `曾参与` instead of `主导` unless confirmed.
- Use `待补充` for missing credentials.

## Capability Pillars

Choose 3-4 capability pillars:

1. Industry expertise
   - Experience in `[industry]`
   - Understanding of `[customer workflow]`
   - Ability to identify high-pain problems

2. Product and technology capability
   - Product definition
   - Prototype/MVP delivery
   - AI/software/workflow implementation

3. Growth and commercialization
   - Go-to-market strategy
   - Content, channel, sales, or partnership growth
   - Customer validation and revenue model design

4. Resource network
   - Academic, industry, media, channel, investor, or ecosystem resources
   - Partnerships that can support pilots, distribution, or credibility

5. Execution and operations
   - Project management
   - Delivery process
   - Team building and customer success

## Team Section Template

Early-stage team wording:

> The early team is led by `[Founder Name]`, with support from `[technical/commercial/operation roles]`. The team currently focuses on `[product definition]`, `[customer validation]`, `[MVP delivery]`, and `[commercial pilots]`. In the next stage, the team plans to add `[roles to hire]` to strengthen `[capability gaps]`.

Chinese version:

> 项目早期团队由 `[创始人姓名]` 牵头，协同 `[技术/商业/运营角色]` 推进。现阶段重点聚焦 `[产品定义]`、`[客户验证]`、`[MVP 交付]` 和 `[商业试点]`。下一阶段计划补充 `[拟招募岗位]`，加强 `[能力缺口]`。

## Competitive Advantage Patterns

Avoid generic claims such as “team is excellent” or “technology is advanced.” Convert team facts into defensible advantages.

Advantage categories:

1. Industry insight advantage
   - The team understands a specific customer workflow better than generic tool providers.

2. Productization advantage
   - The team can turn service know-how into reusable product modules, templates, or workflows.

3. Data/workflow advantage
   - The product captures repeated workflow data and improves recommendations or execution.

4. Channel/resource advantage
   - The team has access to channels, partners, or ecosystem resources that reduce customer acquisition cost.

5. Delivery advantage
   - The team can combine software, service, training, or consulting into a practical adoption path.

6. Trust/compliance advantage
   - The product has credible mechanisms for safety, review, privacy, accuracy, or regulated workflows.

## Competitive Advantage Paragraph

Template:

> The project’s advantage comes from the combination of `[founder/team capability]`, `[product workflow]`, and `[go-to-market/resource network]`. Compared with `[alternative]`, the project is better positioned to deliver `[specific result]` because it can `[mechanism 1]`, `[mechanism 2]`, and `[mechanism 3]`.

Chinese version:

> 项目的竞争优势来自 `[团队能力]`、`[产品工作流]` 和 `[市场/资源网络]` 的组合。相较于 `[替代方案]`，项目更适合解决 `[具体问题]`，因为其能够 `[机制1]`、`[机制2]`，并通过 `[机制3]` 形成可复制交付。

## Slide Titles

Use titles like:
- 团队能力：从行业经验到产品交付
- 创始人能力：业务理解 + 产品交付 + 增长资源
- 竞争优势：把团队经验转化为可复制壁垒
- 为什么是这个团队：洞察、交付与商业化闭环
